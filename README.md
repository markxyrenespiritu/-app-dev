## Top Must-Watch Series

### 1. Breaking Bad
> "I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger."
> — *Walter White*

A high school chemistry teacher diagnosed with terminal cancer turns to cooking methamphetamine to secure his family's financial future. 

* **Genre:** Crime, Drama, Thriller
* **Why Watch:** Unmatched character development and intense suspense.
* **Watch Progress:**
  - [x] Season 1–3
  - [x] Season 4–5
  - [ ] *El Camino* (Sequel Movie)

### 2. Reacher
Based on the best-selling novels, a retired military police investigator travels across the country and finds himself pulled into deadly conspiracies.

* **Key Elements:**
  1. Brutal, realistic hand-to-hand combat.
  2. Sharp detective work and old-school justice.
  3. Zero filler episodes.

### 3. The Boys
A gritty, dark-humor take on what happens when superheroes abuse their superpowers instead of using them for good. It turns the classic superhero genre ~~wholesome~~ **completely chaotic**.

### 4. Peaky Blinders
Set in post-WWI Birmingham, England, this stylish gangster epic follows the ambitious and ruthless Shelby crime family. It is an ==absolute masterpiece== in cinematography and soundtrack.
